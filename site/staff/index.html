      Adding a teammate

      Only the owner can do this, in Supabase.

      1. Open your project at supabase.com and go to Authentication → Users.
      2. Click Add user → Send invitation and enter their email. They get an email to set a password.
      3. They sign in here. To remove someone, delete their user in the same list.

          Add Contrail to your Home Screen

          It opens full screen like an app, and it's the only way iPhone allows phone alerts.

          1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow).
          2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
          3. Open Contrail from your home screen and sign in.
          4. Tap More → Turn on phone alerts.
