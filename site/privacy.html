Privacy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Contrail is a free flight forecast site. We keep what we collect to a minimum. This page explains what happens when you use the site.

What we don't collect

You don't need an account. We don't sell personal information. We never ask for or receive payment details. When you book, you pay the booking site directly.

Your searches

Searches run in your browser using flight records we publish with the site. The airports and dates you search can appear in the page address, for example #BWI-MCO-20261204, so you can share a search. We don't store your searches on a server.

Partners and cookies

  • Travelpayouts. Our affiliate partner. A Travelpayouts script on the site and the links to booking partners use cookies and similar tools so a booking can be credited to Contrail. A booking stays credited for about 30 days after your click.
  • Kiwi.com price widget. The live prices section is provided by Kiwi.com through Travelpayouts. It loads from their servers and sends them the route and dates you searched. If you book, Kiwi.com handles your booking under its own privacy policy.
  • Aviasales. The "Compare on Aviasales" links take you to Aviasales, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts. Our typeface loads from Google, which receives your IP address when it does.
  • GitHub Pages. The site is hosted on GitHub, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Call-back requests

If you ask us to call you back, we receive your name, phone number, and anything else you enter, such as your email, trip and best time to call. We store it in our database provider, Supabase, and only signed-in Contrail staff can see it. We use it only to call you about your trip. Ask us any time and we'll delete it.

If you call us, an AI receptionist may answer first. It tells you that it's an AI and that the call is recorded, and it passes your details to our staff the same way. The AI receptionist runs on xAI's voice service, which processes the call and keeps the recording for about 30 days. Our staff can listen to recordings to check details and improve our service.

Quotes

If we send you a flight quote, it lives at a private link that only works with the code in that link. The quote shows your name, route, travel date, number of travelers and the flight options and prices we found. We record when the quote is opened and which option you tap, so we know when to call you. Quotes aren't listed or searchable anywhere.

Text messages

We may text you about a trip you asked us about, such as a quote or a booking link. Texts come from our staff. Reply STOP or tell us at any time and we won't text you again. Message and data rates may apply. We don't use your number for marketing lists and we don't share it with anyone else for their marketing.

Calendar reminders

Reminders are created in your own calendar app. Contrail doesn't receive or store them.

Children

Contrail isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. For questions or deletion requests, use the call-back form on our home page.
